# O General AC UAE – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

O General AC UAE is the website brand of Optimus General Air Conditions Trading LLC, a Dubai-based supplier and service provider for O General air conditioning systems. The business serves residential and commercial customers with AC sales, installation, maintenance, repairs and technical support.

The redesigned homepage focuses on clearer business positioning, easier product discovery and more direct enquiry pathways for customers comparing AC systems or seeking HVAC services.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Consistent Business Messaging
- Established a consistent Optimus General business identity across the homepage.
- Replaced repetitive O General keyword usage with concise, customer-focused messaging.
- Clarified the company's position as an AC equipment supplier and HVAC service provider.

### 2. Improved Product Discovery and Technical Information
- Organised products into clearer categories, including wall-mounted split, ducted split, cassette, window, ceiling-suspended and VRF systems.
- Added application-focused descriptions to help customers understand the differences between system types.
- Created clearer enquiry pathways for customers interested in specific products and models.

### 3. More Structured Conversion Journey
- Connected product interests with relevant enquiry calls to action.
- Made installation, maintenance and after-sales services easier to discover.
- Improved the visibility of contact information and introduced a persistent mobile conversion bar to support enquiries.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual presentation:** Created a more consistent presentation around the O General AC UAE brand and its HVAC product offering.
- **Layout:** Structured the homepage around business introduction, product categories, services and contact opportunities.
- **Product navigation:** Made the different AC system categories easier to browse and compare.
- **User experience:** Connected product information with relevant enquiry actions to reduce friction in the customer journey.
- **Content consistency:** Reduced repetitive keywords and clarified the distinction between equipment supply and HVAC services.
- **Mobile experience:** Added a persistent mobile conversion bar for easier access to enquiry options.
